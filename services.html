Services

Consultancy and customized professional learning

Flexible support for businesses, professionals and teams, delivered online or at an agreed location.

Business consultancy

One-to-one and organizational consultancy can be arranged for selected business areas.

Import and Export Business

Guidance may cover item selection, supplier sourcing, costing, documentation, letters of credit, freight, customs, taxation, market review and risk mitigation.

C&F and Customs

Practical discussion of business documents, customs procedures, HS code selection, freight calculation, tax and VAT, costing and operational risk.

Commission and Indenting Agency

Support relating to agency arrangements, supplier sourcing, commission processes, documentation, banking procedures and market considerations.

International Tender Business

Structured consultation on international tender processes and relevant business documentation.

Customized corporate training

Organizations can request tailor-made programs that match their participants, objectives and operating context.

Flexible Topics

Accounting, finance, HR, marketing, sales, commercial operations, garments, NGO management, law, IT, banking, administration and leadership.

Flexible Delivery

Programs may be delivered at iPAS facilities, online or on-site at the client's location, subject to agreement.

Practical Methods

Interactive sessions may use demonstrations, discussion, workplace examples and role-playing to connect learning with real situations.
