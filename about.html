About iPAS

Professional advancement through practical learning

We support individuals and organizations with relevant education, workplace skills and professional development services.

Who we are

As a professional training house, iPAS was established in April 2010 with the intention of supporting the emerging financial industry in Bangladesh. We aim to inspire corporate and individual clients to learn valuable skills using established and emerging practices, contributing to sustainable economic growth and greater opportunity.

Our mission

Our mission is to create a strong platform for the financial and industrial sectors and add value as a strategic partner to the business community by:

  • Creating a high level of professionalism in the workplace
  • Arranging effective human capital development programs
  • Promoting professional education locally and internationally
  • Addressing relevant national and international issues

Our vision

To be a leading human capital advancement center and create strong international linkages for skilled industry professionals.
